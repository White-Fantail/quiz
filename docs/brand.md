# Clupio — brand summary

External name: **Clupio (클루피오)**. Repository/internal codename remains `quiz`.

**Follow the clues. Find the fun.**  
**단서를 따라, 재미를 발견해.**

Supporting copy: **Create it. Play it. Share it. / 만들고, 풀고, 함께 즐겨.**

We lead with fun, curiosity and discovery, not exams. Product copy is friendly, short, encouraging and factual. No shaming users for wrong answers.

Service structure: **Clupio Play, Clupio Discover, Clupio Studio**. Packs are collections, Kids is a safety-filtered mode, Connect names future AI/MCP integrations. Pio is a *proposed mascot concept*, not an approved asset. Korean/English localization is a future implementation task.

Do not weaken children’s safeguards, factual accuracy or accessibility to fit the playful tone. Official logos, trademarks, domains and illustration assets have not been validated.
