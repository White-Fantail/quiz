# Product principles

> 문제 제작은 자유롭게. 공개는 즉시. 분류는 자동으로. 노출은 사용자에 맞게. 안전 관리는 지속적으로.

QuizDip is an open quiz platform centered on progressive clues and the joy of figuring things out.

- Anyone can create and publish quizzes. Minimize required creator settings; AI can propose defaults and help craft better clue progression.
- Creators may edit their target audience or intention, but cannot alter platform-assessed safety.
- Distinguish creator intent, inferred audience/difficulty, and child-safety eligibility.
- Ordinary content is available without manual pre-approval where automated checks permit it; Kids exposure requires an affirmative safety decision.
- Uncertain Kids content stays hidden until reviewed or corrected. Content violating core safety rules may be blocked everywhere.
- Use player reports, real play data, error flags and creator revisions to improve quality after publishing.
- Different modes can use words, partial images, audio, videos and geographic clues with different rules and scoring.
- ChatGPT/MCP and web Studio should operate through the same API, permissions and audit trails.
- Take special care with children’s data, privacy, copyrighted materials, political neutrality and source accuracy.

This document states **planned product behavior**, not completion claims.
